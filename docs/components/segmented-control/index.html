Segmented Control

One choice from a few views or options, with a white knob that slides to the picked one. Works as tabs or as a radio group, fully keyboard driven.

A segmented control picks one of a few options. A white knob slides to the picked one. As tabs (the default) it switches a panel; as a radio group it sets an option. The arrow keys move along and pick, and Home and End jump to the ends. The label is required and names the group for screen readers.

Usage

Properties

NameTypeDescription
options{ value: string; label: ReactNode }[]The choices, in order, left to right.
value / defaultValuestringThe picked value. Pass value with onValueChange for a controlled control.
onValueChange(value: string) => voidFires with the new value when the pick changes.
labelstringNames the group for screen readers, like View. Required.
astabs | radiotabs (the default) when it switches a panel, radio when it sets an option.
idstringTies the tabs to their panel: give the panel an id of the form "id-panel" and point its aria-labelledby at "id-value". Defaults to a generated id.

Theming

Works in light and dark (data-theme="dark") and in the Soft style (data-style="soft"). The track and sliding knob take the active theme's surface and accent colors; in Blueprint the control is squared off, in Soft it is a pill with a rounder knob.

Stat cards

A row of KPIs, each with its change against the period before and a sparkline, with a period switch that rolls the numbers and redraws the lines.

Buttons

A pill button in the library's variants and sizes, with built-in busy, done and pressed states.
