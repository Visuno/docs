Toast stack

Agent notifications that stack into a deck and fan out on hover.

A toast stack collects notifications into a deck: the newest sits in front, older ones peek out behind it. Hover the deck — or move the focus into it — and it fans out into a readable list; move away and it folds back up. Each toast takes an icon, a tone for the badge colour, a title, an optional detail line and a time. Pass the toasts oldest-first; dismiss one with its close button.

Usage

Properties

NameTypeDescription
toastsStackToast[]Oldest first. The last one sits in front.
onDismiss(id) => voidCalled when a toast's close button is pressed. Remove it from toasts and it slides away.
expandedbooleanFans the stack out. Leave it out to fan it while the pointer is over it.
maxnumberHow many toasts stay mounted. Older ones drop off the back. Defaults to 6.
toast.idstring | numberIdentity of the toast; a new one rises in, a removed one slides away.
toast.iconIconNameThe icon in the round badge.
toast.toneneutral | ok | warn | bad | merge | blue | purple | …Colours the badge. Defaults to neutral.
toast.titlestringThe bold first line.
toast.detailstringThe quiet line under the title.
toast.timestringA short time like now or 2m, shown until the close button takes its place.

Theming

Works in light and dark (data-theme="dark") and in the Soft style (data-style="soft"). Each toast sits on a surface with the toast shadow and its badge tint comes from the theme tokens, so the deck reads against the dark stage; in Soft the cards are rounder. The fan-out and the arrival and dismiss motion are tuned down when the viewer prefers reduced motion.
